Vision

A small factory in Saudi Arabia that makes one sausage well: Caucasian-style cooked-smoked beef servelat, sliced and eaten cold.

Mission

We are building a small halal line in the Kingdom. One flagship. Beef, natural spices, a Caucasian cure, no hydrocolloids on the flagship.

Local beef where it holds quality. A short list of imported casings and spices. Smoke for flavour, not theatre. Real batches, not a catalogue of SKUs.

How we work

Beef only. No pork. Halal. Made in Saudi. The product is cooked-smoked servelat in the Caucasian tradition, not Swiss cervelat and not a snack brand.
