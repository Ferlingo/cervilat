The sausage

Servelat — cervilat — is a cooked-smoked beef sausage with a coarse grain and a quiet spice. Ours follows a Caucasian cure: beef, natural spices, no hydrocolloids on the flagship.

It is made to be sliced, packed, and eaten cold. Smoke is for flavour, not for theatre.

The place

Cervilat is a project of NAAS Holding, Saudi Arabia. We are building a small halal line in the Kingdom. Local beef where it holds quality, a short list of imported casings and spices, and a process sized for real batches — not a catalogue of SKUs.
